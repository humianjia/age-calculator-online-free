About This Social Security Calculator

This free Social Security calculator estimates your monthly retirement benefit using the SSA's bend-point formula. Enter your average earnings and choose when to claim — at 62 (reduced), 67 (full retirement age), or 70 (maximum). See how your claiming age dramatically affects your lifetime benefit.
